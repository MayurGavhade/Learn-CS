Operators

5. Left Shift Operator(<<)
e.g. (10)10 = (1010)2 , 10 << 1
step: 10 << 1 = 1010 << 1 = 10100 = (20)10

General point: a << b = a * 2^b (important to remamber).

6. Right Shift Operator(>>)
e.g. (10)10 = (1010)2 , 10 >> 1
step: 10 >> 1 = 1010 >> 1 = 101 = (5)10

General point: a >> b = a / 2^b (important to remamber).
